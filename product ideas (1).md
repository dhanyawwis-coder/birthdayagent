Idea: This is an AI agent for sending  
 birthday wishes \- posters to parent groups in whatsapp.                                

Purpose: 

Currently our school community has problems in collecting birthdata, remembering the dates and quickly making a birthday poster featuring photos of the learners and sending them to the parent whatsapp group. We want to automate this. 

Major Tasks:

1. Collect birthday date of learners   
2. Store birthday details (name, birth date, age) [WWIS BIRTHDAY LIST](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_dl8wLyrLZmQKQQLVVRHYJIHZQtgyk9b/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=103104261457511776488&rtpof=true&sd=true)  
3. Get 10 birthday templates from school designers and store it  
4. Before seven days to birth day date, send reminders to organizing team to get photos of the students  
5. Design the poster  
6. Send for review to organizer  
7. Once approved, post it on the birthday poster in whatsapp group  
8. Repeat this for all  birthdays and all learners 

     

Features:   Auto reminder notification

                    Poster templates (simple, cute, aesthetic, etc.)

                   Custom colors and fonts

                    Add photo option

                    Download as image/PDF

Use case:  The AI Birthday Poster Automation System is designed for schools, and event organizing teams to automatically generate and distribute birthday posters for students without manual intervention.

Version 1 :

1.Store birthday data

2.Basic reminder system

3.One or two poster templates

4.Manual WhatsApp posting

Version 2

1.System chooses different designs based on student class (i-play,i-discover,i-lead)

2.If a birthday is coming and a photo is missing, the system sends a reminder to upload it.

3.System creates the birthday poster using correct template \+ student details.

4.Admin checks the poster and approves or rejects it.

5.After approval, the poster is sent to the parent group.

Version 3:     
   
1\. Fully automated poster generation                                                       
                                                                                                                                 2.AI-enhanced design customization

3.Automatic WhatsApp posting on birthday

4.Dashboard to track all upcoming birthdays  
   
5.Annual birthday report system 

Version 4: Multi Channel Publishing

1. Multi-channel publishing including WhatsApp, email, school app, Instagram, and digital notice boards.

2. AI-powered personalization with smart design adjustments, milestone highlights, and unique birthday messages.

3. Advanced analytics dashboard with engagement tracking, predictive alerts, and automated annual reports.
